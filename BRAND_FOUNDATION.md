# CourseKin Brand Foundation

**Created:** May 25, 2026
**Status:** Initial working identity for v2 product development; refine after user testing and formal brand clearance.

## Brand Idea

CourseKin is a supportive academic course companion. Its visual language should feel calm, capable, organized, and encouraging: serious enough for coursework, warm enough for an ongoing student relationship.

## Color Palette

| Role | Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `--ck-ink` | `#12343B` | Navigation, strong headings, trust anchor |
| Primary teal | `--ck-primary` | `#087F75` | Main actions, selected state, progress, brand mark |
| Teal hover | `--ck-primary-hover` | `#06665F` | Interactive hover and active feedback |
| Soft mint | `--ck-primary-soft` | `#E7F5F1` | Chips, low-emphasis highlights, supportive surfaces |
| Mint border | `--ck-primary-border` | `#B7DED6` | Branded outlines and gentle separators |
| Warm gold | `--ck-accent` | `#E5A83F` | Milestones, earned progress, premium emphasis |
| Gold wash | `--ck-accent-soft` | `#FFF3DC` | Low-emphasis milestone or plan badges |
| Page surface | `--ck-bg` | `#F6FAF8` | Marketing and general page background |
| App surface | `--ck-shell-bg` | `#EDF5F2` | Dashboard shell background |

Dark-theme equivalents are defined in `frontend/app/globals.css` and should be tested whenever new branded components are added.

## Usage Rules

- Use teal for actions that help a student move forward: generating a reviewer, creating a course, confirming a plan, and viewing progress.
- Use gold sparingly for earned milestones, assessment runway highlights, or paid-plan emphasis. It should not compete with primary actions.
- Use ink for stable navigation and strong text, supporting a dependable academic feeling.
- Keep warning, error, and success semantic colors separate from branding when they communicate system status.
- Favor clear, spacious layouts and concise supportive copy over playful clutter.

## Current Application

- The public landing page carries the wordmark, teal action treatment, soft branded background wash, and companion positioning.
- Shared buttons, badges, progress bars, dashboard navigation, and topbar inherit the initial identity.
- The pricing surface reserves gold for plan emphasis while primary calls to action remain teal.
